      About the investigation
      How the 3D view is drawn
      • The sample strip uses the sample length, width and thickness from tests.csv. Grid squares are 1 cm.
      • The flame sits on the luminous region, which runs from the trailing edge to the flame position measured in the camera images, one value per second, measured from the igniter coil along the sample. Its width is set from the luminous pixel area and the image scale.
      • The images give no height above the sample. The flame height (set by the size factor), its blue, yellow and orange colouring, its flicker and its lean with the airflow are drawing choices, not measurements.
      • The igniter coil lights up while igniter_on = 1. Tracers move at the measured airflow.
      • In the Earth environment the sample stands upright, as in NASA's upward flame test, and the flame front moves at the published average 1-g spread rate up to the published burn length. That playback is a reconstruction from averages, not a recording.
      • The blue Earth outline spans the 1-g burn length of the same sample. The orange outline spans the reported burn length in low gravity.
